Alma's Family Restaurant is the family diner inside Grand Bay Mall in Channel-Port aux Basques — omelettes and French toast to start the day, roast turkey suppers and hot sandwiches at lunch and dinner, and Newfoundland seafood done right.

Breakfast Favourites

All-day-style breakfast with omelettes, French toast and the classics — the way a proper family diner starts the morning.

Turkey & Hot Sandwiches

Roast turkey supper, plus hot turkey and hot beef sandwiches — hearty, home-style plates that keep locals coming back.

From Local Waters

Cod nuggets & fries, fish & chips, halibut steak and a fisherman's platter — fresh-tasting seafood in the heart of a port town.
